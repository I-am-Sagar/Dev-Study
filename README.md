### Our Key Principles 

Our vision is to create a learning system that bridges the gap between academia and industry by providing practical technical education. 

#### Async Videos

* **Course Lectures:** Learners learn the best way with a multimodal approach where words, pictures and audio are presented together. So, we would want to have videos where the narration complements the visuals and have some text on the screen as well.

#### Practice Problems 

*  **Immediate Feedback:** All practice problems will provide the learner an immediate feedback. Feedbacks will guide the learner to improvement. 
* **Repeated Attempts**: Make the learner do similar exercises again and again till the point the pattern used in that problem gets into their subconscious.
* **Ignite Interest**: Make exercises interesting enough that the learner would want to put himself in an uncomfortable zone deliberately. If the learner doesn't want to do something, we won't be able to make them do that thing. We would need to ignite this longing in him.

#### Support Material 

* **Notes**: Students should have two types of reading material: 
    * *Revision Notes:* This is the material that contains what is covered in the class. This helps the student revise the lecture contents quickly. 
    * *Extra Notes:* This is the material that supplements the learning of the student. This contains extra content, out of the box topics, etc.
* **Personalized Arena**: An arena with missions/gamification for efficient and interesting problem solving habit. Based on the performance of students at various places - course practice questions, previous arena questions, etc., arena will recommend the questions in a way that tries to constantly fix the weakpoints of students in a personalized way.
